# Statement of Work — Platform Modernisation

**Between:** Northwind Digital Ltd. ("Supplier") and **Initech LLC** ("Client")

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

**Currency:** All amounts are stated and payable in **United States Dollars (USD)**.

## 1. Engagement

Initech LLC engages the Supplier to modernise its order-management platform across
three sequential delivery phases, billed per milestone.

## 2. Payment

All payments are made via **PayPal** to the Supplier's nominated account. The
Client's authorised PayPal payer address for this Statement of Work is
**ap@initech.example.com**.

## 3. Milestones and Fees

- **Section 4.1 (MS-01):** Discovery & Requirements — **$1,200.00 USD**, due on or
  before **October 10, 2026**.
- **Section 4.2 (MS-02):** Implementation Sprint 1 — **$1,800.00 USD**, due on or
  before **November 10, 2026**.
- **Section 4.3 (MS-03):** Final Delivery & Handover — **$900.00 USD**, due on or
  before **December 10, 2026**.

## 4. Partial Payments

**Section 5.2:** Partial payments are **permitted** for MS-02 and MS-03 only,
provided the outstanding balance is settled within the grace period. Any shortfall
remaining after the grace period is treated as an underpayment.

## 5. Late Payment

**Section 6.1:** A late fee of **5.00%** of the milestone amount applies to any
payment received more than **5 days** past the milestone due date (the grace
period for every milestone under this Statement of Work is five (5) calendar days).
