# Master Software Development Agreement

**Between:** Northwind Digital Ltd. ("Client-facing Studio") and **Acme Corp** ("Client")

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

**Currency:** All amounts in this Agreement are stated and payable in **United States Dollars (USD)**.

## 1. Engagement

Acme Corp engages the Studio to design, build, and deliver the Phase 1 backend
architecture for the Acme Commerce Platform, on a fixed-milestone basis.

## 2. Payment Mechanics

All milestone payments shall be made to the Studio via **PayPal** to the business
account nominated by the Studio. The Client's authorised PayPal payer address for
this Agreement is **sb-buyer@personal.example.com**.

## 3. Milestones and Fees

**Section 3.1:** Client shall pay **$1,000.00 USD** upon Phase 1 delivery on or
before **October 15, 2026** (Milestone Code **MS-01** — "Phase 1: Backend
Architecture Delivery").

## 4. Late Payment and Penalties

**Section 4.2:** Any payment received below the milestone amount or more than
**3 days** past the due date incurs a mandatory **5.00%** administrative late fee
on the full milestone balance. The grace period for MS-01 is **three (3) calendar
days** following the due date.

## 5. Partial Payments

**Section 5.4:** Partial payment of any milestone is **FORBIDDEN**. A transfer
received for less than the full milestone amount is treated as an underpayment and
the outstanding balance becomes immediately due.

## 6. Currency

Payments made in any currency other than USD shall be flagged as a currency
mismatch and are not deemed compliant until the full USD equivalent is received.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
