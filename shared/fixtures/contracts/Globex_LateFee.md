# Consulting Services Agreement

**Between:** Northwind Digital Ltd. ("Consultant") and **Globex Industries Inc.** ("Client")

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026

**Currency:** All fees are quoted and payable in **United States Dollars (USD)**.

## 1. Scope

Globex Industries engages the Consultant to perform a comprehensive third-party
security and compliance audit of the Globex customer data platform, concluding
with a written audit report.

## 2. Invoicing and Payment

The Client shall pay each fee via **PayPal** to the Consultant's nominated business
account. The Client's authorised PayPal payer address under this Agreement is
**finance@globex.example.com**.

## 3. Fees and Milestone

**Section 3.1:** A single fixed fee of **$2,500.00 USD** is due upon delivery of
the **Q3 Security Audit Report** (Milestone Code **MS-01**) on or before
**November 1, 2026**.

## 4. Late Payment

**Section 5 (Late Payment):** A late fee of **7.00%** of the invoice total shall
apply to any payment received after the due date. **No grace period** is granted
for this engagement; the due date is strict.

## 5. Partial Payments

**Section 5.3:** The Client may not settle this milestone in instalments; the full
amount is due as a single payment.

## 6. Currency

**Section 6.1:** All payments must be settled in **United States Dollars (USD)**.
A payment received in any other currency is not deemed compliant until the full USD
equivalent has been received.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA.
